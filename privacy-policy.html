Privacy Policy

App: Speed Test Lite (com.speedtestlite) • Developer: SK Studio • Effective Date: October 25, 2026
Summary: Speed Test Lite does not collect, record, track, store, or sell any personal data. It contains no advertisements, no tracking SDKs, no analytics, and requires no user account or login.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Speed Test Lite Android application developed by SK Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a zero-tracking, privacy-first experience for our users.

2. Information Collection and Use

Speed Test Lite does not collect or transmit personal information.

  • No Personal Data: We do not ask for, collect, store, or transmit your name, phone number, email address, device identifiers, or contact lists.
  • No Account Required: The application functions completely without any registration, account creation, or login.
  • No Analytics or Ads: There are no third-party tracking libraries, advertising networks, crash-reporting SDKs, or analytics trackers embedded in the application.

3. Network Telemetry and Speed Testing

To measure network speed, Ping, Jitter, Download rate, and Upload rate, the app sends standard HTTP requests to public speed-test endpoints (such as Cloudflare Speed Test):

  • These requests transfer test payloads to calculate your current throughput (Mbps) and latency (ms).
  • The network diagnostics displayed on screen (such as local IPv4 address, gateway, network interface, and Wi-Fi / 4G / 5G status) are queried locally on your device via standard Android system APIs.
  • None of this technical diagnostic data is saved, logged, or uploaded to any remote server or third party.

4. Android Permissions

Speed Test Lite requests only the minimum normal install-time permissions necessary to test internet speeds and inspect your active connection:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to send and receive test data across the internet to compute speed metrics.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify whether the device is currently connected to a network (Wi-Fi, Mobile Cellular, or Ethernet) prior to running a test.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Required to display local Wi-Fi connection info (such as local IP address and Wi-Fi link speed).

The app does not request any sensitive or dangerous permissions, such as Location, Storage, Camera, Microphone, Phone State, or Contacts.

5. Third-Party Services

The app connects directly to publicly available speed test infrastructure (e.g. Cloudflare) strictly to conduct bandwidth and latency measurement. These servers receive standard HTTP connection metadata (such as your public IP address) strictly as required to fulfill standard TCP/IP networking, in accordance with standard internet protocols.

6. Children's Privacy

Speed Test Lite does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for all audiences.
