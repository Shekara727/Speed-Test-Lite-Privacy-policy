Section 01

Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the Speed Test Lite mobile application (package identifier com.speedtestlite) created by SK Studio. Our application is engineered to measure internet performance—Ping, Jitter, Download Mbps, Upload Mbps, and estimated 1 GB file download time—without compromising your privacy.

Section 02

Information We Do Not Collect

Speed Test Lite does not collect, log, transmit, or sell any personal or sensitive user data:

  • No Identity or Contact Data: We do not request or access your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or account credentials.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not request GPS (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` or `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) or track your physical whereabouts.
  • No Analytics or Advertising Identifiers: The application contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting agents, or advertising libraries.
Section 03

How Speed Testing & Local Diagnostics Work

When you tap Start Test or view your connection status:

  • Bandwidth & Latency Measurement: The app performs direct HTTPS requests to Cloudflare's public speed-test endpoints (speed.cloudflare.com) to measure Ping (ms), Jitter (ms), Download speed (Mbps), and Upload speed (Mbps). Only standard ephemeral HTTP test buffers are exchanged.
  • Local Network Inspection: Connection status (`Connected` / `Disconnected`), active transport (`Wi-Fi`, `5G`, `4G`, `Ethernet`), local IPv4 address, default gateway, network interface name, and carrier label are read locally from Android system services and displayed solely on your device screen. This information is never transmitted off your device.
Section 04

Android Permissions Explained

Speed Test Lite requests only three normal, install-time Android network permissions:

Permission Purpose in Speed Test Lite
android.permission.INTERNET Required to perform real HTTP/HTTPS Ping, Download, and Upload speed tests.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required to detect whether the device is online and identify Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G, or Ethernet transport.
android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Required to display local Wi-Fi IPv4 address and gateway diagnostics on screen.
