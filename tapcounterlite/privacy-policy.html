Overview & App Identity

Tap Counter Lite (Package ID: com.tapcounterlite) is developed and published by SK Studio as a lightweight, 100% offline utility application for Android. We respect your privacy completely: this application does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or device data.

2

Zero Data Collection & Zero Storage

Tap Counter Lite operates strictly in temporary memory while the app is open on your screen:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or account login.
  • No Persistent Storage: Counter values start at 0 on every launch and are never written to local databases, files, or cloud servers.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: The app contains zero analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or background tracking scripts.
3

Device Permissions

Tap Counter Lite declares and requests zero Android permissions in its manifest:

  • No INTERNET or network state access
  • No CAMERA, microphone, or photo/media access
  • No location, contacts, Bluetooth, or background service permissions
4

Third-Party Libraries & Advertising

Tap Counter Lite is built exclusively with native Android OS framework APIs with zero third-party runtime libraries. There are no advertisements, no ad networks, and no third-party code capable of harvesting user behavior.

5

Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Compliance)

Because Tap Counter Lite collects no personal data or device identifiers from any user of any age, it is completely safe for general audiences and complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families policies.

6

Security & Google Play Data Safety

Because the application has no network access (INTERNET permission is absent) and stores no data on disk (android:allowBackup="false"), your usage cannot be intercepted, leaked, or accessed by external parties.
