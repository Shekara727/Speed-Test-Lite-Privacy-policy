Overview & App Identity

Tap Counter Lite (Package ID: com.tapcounterlite) is developed and published by SK Studio as a lightweight tally and tap counter utility for Android. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, how advertising is displayed, and the terms governing your use of the application.

2

Personal & Counter Data (Zero Storage by SK Studio)

SK Studio does not collect, store, or upload any personal information or counter history on our own servers:

  • No Account or Registration: You never need to provide your name, email address, phone number, or contact list.
  • No Counter Persistence: Counter values exist only in temporary memory during your active session and reset to 0 on every new launch.
  • No Camera, Microphone, or Contacts Access: The app never accesses your private files, photos, camera, or microphone.
3

Advertising Service & Placement Disclosure

To keep Tap Counter Lite free for all users, the application uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements.

  • Ad Provider Used: Google AdMob (Google LLC).
  • Where Ads Are Displayed: A single, non-intrusive Banner Ad is placed strictly inside a dedicated container at the bottom edge of the main counter screen, separated from the central +, − Minus, and ↻ Reset buttons so it never interrupts your counting workflow.
  • No Pop-up or Lock-Screen Ads: The app does not display disruptive out-of-app ads or unexpected full-screen interruptions while you are tapping.
4

Data Processed by Google AdMob

For the sole purpose of loading and displaying the bottom banner advertisement, preventing fraud, and measuring ad performance, the integrated Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and transmit certain device-level signals to Google:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and basic device parameters (device model, OS version, screen dimensions, language).
  • Coarse Location (IP-based): Approximate country/region inferred from your network IP address to serve relevant regional ads.
  • Ad Interactions & Diagnostics: Banner ad impressions, clicks, and basic SDK performance/crash diagnostics related to ad delivery.

All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and governed by Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5

Device Permissions Used

Tap Counter Lite requests only standard install-time permissions required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load the bottom banner ad:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Used strictly to fetch banner ad content from Google AdMob servers.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used to check whether a network connection is available before requesting a banner ad.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Used by Google Play Services for advertising measurement, frequency capping, and fraud prevention in compliance with Google Play policies.

The counting functionality itself works 100% offline even if your device has no internet connection.

6

Your Privacy Choices & Opt-Out Controls

You have full control over how the Google Advertising ID is used on your Android device:

  • You can Reset your Advertising ID or Delete your Advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization anytime by opening your device’s Settings → Google → All services → Ads.
  • When you opt out or delete your Advertising ID, the bottom banner will only display non-personalized, contextual advertisements.
