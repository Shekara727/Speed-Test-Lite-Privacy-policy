01

Overview and Scope

SK Studio ("we", "our", or "us") developed the QR Maker Lite mobile application (package identifier com.qrmakerlite, version 1.1.0, Build 11) as a free, ad-supported QR code generator and scanner for Android. This Privacy Policy explains our strict zero-server architecture, how QR codes are processed on your phone, what permissions are used, and how third-party advertisements operate via Google AdMob.

02

No Developer Servers, No Database, and No Personal or Location Data Collection

SK Studio does not own or operate any backend servers or databases for QR Maker Lite, and we do not collect, store, read, or transmit any personal information from your device.

  • No Server or Cloud Database: QR Maker Lite has no developer backend server and no cloud database. Nothing you type, generate, or scan is ever sent to SK Studio.
  • No Location Permission or Tracking: QR Maker Lite never requests or accesses GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular location permissions. We never ask for your location.
  • 100% On-Device QR Code Generation (Text, Website Links, Wi-Fi): Any plain text, website URL, or Wi-Fi network configuration (SSID, security type, and password) you enter is encoded into an ISO/IEC 18004 QR code bitmap strictly inside your phone's volatile memory (RAM).
  • 100% On-Device Camera & Picture Scanning: Live camera frames and QR pictures selected via the Photo Picker are decoded locally on your phone with animated reticle tracking.
  • No Login or User Account & Ephemeral Phone Number Handling: The application never requires account registration. Any optional phone number (tel:) you enter to generate a QR code or scan from a QR code is processed 100% ephemerally in device RAM in real time solely for app functionality and is never stored on any server or shared with third parties.
03

Device Permissions, Gallery Saving, and Picture Picker

QR Maker Lite uses only the following standard Android system permissions and zero-permission system pickers:

Permission / System Feature Purpose & How It Works on Your Device
Camera Permission
(android.permission.CAMERA)		 Requested only when you open the Scan QR tab so the camera can detect and decode QR codes in real time. No photos or videos are ever saved or uploaded.
Save to Gallery
(Scoped MediaStore)		 When you tap Save to Gallery, the generated QR code PNG image is saved directly to your device Gallery (Pictures/QRMakerLite) without reading any of your existing photos.
Save to Files
(Storage Access Framework)		 When you tap Save to Files, Android's system file saver allows you to pick a folder on your phone to save the QR code PNG file.
Pick QR Picture
(Android Photo Picker)		 When you tap Pick QR Picture on the scanner screen, Android's built-in Photo Picker lets you select a single QR code image from your Gallery to decode offline.
Internet, Network State & Advertising ID Used exclusively by the integrated Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to request and display Banner and Interstitial advertisements.
04

Website Links, Phone Calls, and UPI Payment Confirmation Prompts

Whenever a scanned QR code contains an external action, QR Maker Lite never triggers it automatically. Instead, it shows a confirmation pop-up first:

  • Website Links (http:// / https://): Shows the full link and destination domain host first, and asks if you want to open it in your browser (Open in Browser).
  • Phone Numbers (tel: — Ephemeral & Optional): Processed ephemerally in memory only when you optionally generate or scan a phone QR code; shows the phone number first and asks if you want to call it (Call Number opens your phone dialer, and the number is never shared or stored).
  • UPI Payment QR Codes (upi://pay): Shows the payee UPI ID, name, and amount first, and asks if you want to make a payment (Pay with UPI App hands off to your installed UPI app). QR Maker Lite never accesses banking credentials or UPI PINs.
05

Official Google AdMob Advertising Disclosure

To keep QR Maker Lite free of charge, the application uses the official Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK provided by Google LLC to display advertisements in two placements:

  • Bottom Banner Ad: A banner advertisement displayed at the bottom edge of the application screen.
  • Full-Screen Interstitial Ad: An interstitial advertisement displayed when the application opens and at periodic intervals during active usage, which can be closed at any time.
  • Official Google AdMob SDK Data Disclosure: While SK Studio does not collect or store any user data on its own servers, the third-party Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK may automatically collect and process certain device-level identifiers and diagnostics—including the Android Advertising ID, device/app interaction events (such as ad impressions and clicks), crash/performance diagnostics, and IP address for general ad delivery, frequency capping, analytics, and fraud/security prevention—in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services.
  • How to Opt Out or Reset Your Advertising ID: You can opt out of personalized ads, reset your Advertising ID, or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
06

Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

Because SK Studio maintains no servers and no databases, no user QR codes, text inputs, Wi-Fi passwords, or scan histories are ever stored or retained by us. All QR data remains strictly in temporary device RAM while on screen and is erased immediately when you tap Clear, tap Scan Again, or close the app. Saved QR code PNG images in your Gallery or Files remain under your sole control on your device and can be deleted by you at any time.
