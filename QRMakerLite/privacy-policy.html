01

Overview and Scope

SK Studio ("we", "our", or "us") built the QR Maker Lite mobile application (package identifier com.qrmakerlite) as an ad-supported utility application for Android. This Privacy Policy describes how QR Maker Lite accesses device permissions, processes QR code data, interacts with external applications, and serves advertisements.

02

QR Code Generation and Scanning Data

We do not collect, store, or transmit the contents of the QR codes you generate or scan to our servers.

  • QR Code Generation (Text, Website URLs, Wi-Fi Networks): Any plain text, website link, or Wi-Fi network configuration (SSID, security type, and password) you enter is encoded into a standard ISO/IEC 18004 QR code bitmap strictly within your device's volatile memory (RAM). Passwords and user inputs are never logged or uploaded.
  • Live Camera QR Scanning & Auto-Tracking: When you scan a QR code using your device camera, the optical decoder detects and tracks the QR code position locally on your screen. Decoded content is displayed only to you in real time.
  • No User Accounts Required: QR Maker Lite does not require account registration, sign-in, or personal profile creation.
03

Device Permissions, Gallery Saving, and Picture Picker

QR Maker Lite requests only the permissions and system interfaces necessary to deliver its core features:

Permission / System API Why We Use It & How It Works
Camera Permission
(android.permission.CAMERA)		 Requested at runtime when you open the Scan QR screen. Camera frames are analyzed in real time on your device solely to locate and decode QR codes. No photographs or videos are recorded, stored, or transmitted.
Save to Gallery
(Android MediaStore)		 When you tap Save to Gallery, the generated QR code PNG is saved directly to your device's photo gallery (Pictures/QRMakerLite) using Android's scoped MediaStore API without requiring broad access to your existing photos.
Save to Files
(Storage Access Framework)		 When you tap Save to Files, Android's system document saver (ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT) allows you to choose a specific folder and filename for your exported PNG image.
Pick QR Picture
(Android Photo Picker)		 When you tap Pick QR Picture on the scanner screen, the system Photo Picker opens so you can select a saved QR code image from your phone to decode. The app accesses only the single image you select.
Share QR
(Native Android Sharesheet)		 When you tap Share QR, the generated PNG is written to a temporary app-private cache file and shared via Android's native Sharesheet with temporary read-only permission.
04

Website Links, Phone Calls, and UPI Payment Actions

When a scanned QR code contains an actionable link, phone number, or payment address, QR Maker Lite displays the decoded information in a preview pop-up first and asks for your confirmation before launching any external application:

  • Website Links (http:// and https://): Displays the complete URL and destination domain host first. Your external web browser is opened only when you tap Open in Browser.
  • Phone Numbers (tel:): Displays the phone number and asks if you want to place a call. Tapping Call Number opens your phone's default dialer application with the number pre-filled so you can initiate the call.
  • UPI Payment Codes (upi://pay): Displays the recipient UPI ID, payee name, and amount (if specified) and asks if you want to proceed. Tapping Pay with UPI App hands the payment request to your chosen installed UPI application. QR Maker Lite never accesses, processes, or stores banking details or UPI PINs.
05

Advertising and How Ads Work in the Application

To keep QR Maker Lite free for all users, the application displays advertisements in the following placements:

  • Bottom Banner Advertisement: A standard banner advertisement displayed at the bottom edge of the application interface.
  • Interstitial Advertisement: A full-screen advertisement displayed when the application opens and at periodic intervals during active usage, which can be closed at any time using the close button.
  • How Advertising Data Is Handled: Advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) may use your device's Advertising ID, coarse IP-based location, device model, and ad interaction metrics to deliver relevant advertisements, limit how often the same ad is shown (frequency capping), measure ad performance, and detect invalid traffic or fraud in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.
  • Managing Your Ad Preferences: You can opt out of personalized advertising, reset your Advertising ID, or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your device's system settings under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
06

Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

QR Maker Lite does not maintain external databases of user-generated QR codes or scan histories. All QR inputs and decoded results remain in temporary device memory only while displayed on screen and are cleared immediately when you tap Clear, tap Scan Again, or exit the application. Any QR code PNG images you save using Save to Gallery or Save to Files are stored locally on your device and can be deleted by you at any time.
