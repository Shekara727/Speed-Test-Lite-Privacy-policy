01

Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the QR Maker Lite Android mobile application (package identifier com.qrmakerlite) developed and published by SK Studio. It discloses how our application handles user and device data in strict compliance with Google Play User Data policies.

02

Information Collection and Use

QR Maker Lite does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal or sensitive user data on external servers. All operations happen locally on your Android device:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We never ask for or access your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or device identifiers.
  • Offline QR Generation (Text, Website URLs, Wi-Fi Credentials): When you generate a QR code for plain text, a website link, or a Wi-Fi network (SSID, password, and encryption type), the QR bitmap is computed strictly inside your device's volatile memory (RAM). Wi-Fi passwords and text inputs are never logged, stored in any database, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Offline QR Scanning (Camera & Uploaded Phone Images): When you scan a physical QR code or select a saved QR image from your device, the optical decoding runs 100% offline on your phone.
03

Device Permissions and Sensitive APIs

QR Maker Lite adheres to the principle of least privilege and requests only the single hardware permission needed for live QR scanning:

Permission / API Purpose & Data Handling
android.permission.CAMERA Requested at runtime only when you open the Scan QR tab. Live camera frames are analyzed in temporary memory to detect QR codes and are immediately discarded. No photos or videos are recorded or saved.
Storage Access Framework (ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT / ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT) Used without requesting broad storage permissions. Allows you to explicitly pick a single QR image file to decode or choose a destination folder to save a generated PNG.
Native Sharesheet (QrShareProvider) When you tap Share QR, the generated PNG is written to a temporary app-private cache path (cacheDir/shared_qr/qr-code.png) with temporary read-only URI access for your chosen sharing app.
android.permission.INTERNET Not requested. The app has zero network capability and cannot connect to the internet.
04

External Website Links & Safe Link Preview

When a scanned QR code contains an http:// or https:// URL, QR Maker Lite displays the full URL and highlights the destination domain host in a preview pop-up first. The application does not contain an embedded WebView and never opens websites automatically.

A website is launched only when you explicitly tap Open in Browser, which hands the URL to your default external web browser via a standard Android ACTION_VIEW intent. Once opened in your external browser, that website's own privacy policy governs your visit.

05

Data Sharing and Third-Party Parties

We do not share, sell, transfer, or disclose any user or device data with any third party under any circumstances.

  • Zero Third-Party Libraries: Built exclusively with standard Android OS APIs.
  • Zero Advertising: Contains no banner ads, interstitials, or Advertising ID access.
  • Zero Analytics or Telemetry: Contains no analytics SDKs, behavioral trackers, or remote crash reporters.
06

Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Because QR Maker Lite does not collect or store personal data or maintain a scan/generation history database, there is no stored user data on our servers to retain or delete. Temporary in-memory QR previews and scan results are erased immediately when you tap Clear, tap Scan Again, or close the app. Any PNG images you explicitly save to your phone remain under your full control and can be deleted at any time from your device storage.

07

Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

QR Maker Lite is a general-audience utility tool and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, we will publish the updated version on this page and revise the Effective Date above.
