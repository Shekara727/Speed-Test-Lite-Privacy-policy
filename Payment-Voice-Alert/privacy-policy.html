Privacy Policy — Payment Voice Alert

Application Name: Payment Voice Alert
Package Name: com.paymentvoice.alert
Developer / Studio: SK Studio
Effective Date: November 2026 (Version 1.0)

Payment Voice Alert (com.paymentvoice.alert), developed by SK Studio, is an independent Android utility application that detects supported incoming (Credit) and outgoing (Debit) payment notifications on your device and announces transaction details aloud via Android Text-to-Speech (TTS).

1. Notification Access & Payment Detection

With your explicit permission via Android's NotificationListenerService, Payment Voice Alert inspects incoming status bar notifications solely to recognize supported Credit and Debit payment alerts from recognized banks and UPI payment applications.

  • Strict Non-Payment Filtering: One-Time Passwords (OTPs), verification codes, promotional messages, account balance inquiries, failed transaction alerts, and personal chat messages are automatically ignored before any storage or voice announcement.
  • No Direct Bank Account Access: Payment Voice Alert does not connect to bank servers, does not read SMS inbox databases, and never initiates or modifies financial transactions.

2. Voice Announcement Processing

Detected Credit and Debit transaction amounts and bank/app names are converted into spoken voice announcements in English, Hindi, or Kannada using your device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine. Voice synthesis occurs locally on your device.

3. Floating Payment Overlay

When you grant the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW ("Display over other apps") permission, Payment Voice Alert displays a temporary visual banner summarizing the detected Credit or Debit transaction. You can configure or disable the overlay at any time in Settings.

4. Local Data Storage & Optional Account Settings Synchronization

  • All notification-derived transaction history is stored exclusively on your device inside an app-private Room SQLite database (payment_voice_alert.db).
  • Raw SMS or bank notification text is never uploaded to Cloud Firestore.
  • When you sign in with an account, only your user profile and non-sensitive app preferences (such as selected language, speech rate, voice volume, and alert toggles) are synchronized under users/{uid}.
  • Guest Demo Mode uses temporary synthetic sample data only and never reads or processes real notifications.

5. Strict Security Boundaries (What We Never Collect)

Payment Voice Alert never collects, requests, stores, or transmits:

  • UPI PINs, MPINs, or ATM PINs
  • One-Time Passwords (OTPs) or 2FA codes
  • NetBanking usernames or passwords
  • Debit card or credit card numbers, CVV, or expiry dates
  • Advertising IDs, behavioral tracking profiles, or third-party ad telemetry

6. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

You retain complete control over your data. At any time in Settings → Account & Privacy Settings, you can:

  • Use Clear Local Transaction History to wipe all locally recorded payment alert records on your device.
  • Use Clear Export Cache & Temporary Files to delete temporary PDF/CSV files.
  • Use Delete Account to permanently delete your cloud-synced profile and preferences (users/{uid}), clear your local credentials, and remove all local transaction records.
